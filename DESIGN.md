# Design System: Gem Infotech

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The local field guide"**

The selected direction makes local technology support approachable through clear service routes, plain language and generous bright space. Bold forest-green headings and warm gold actions connect the page to the supplied Gem Infotech logo. The white opening leads into a dark green approach section, then a quiet service directory, local coverage and a practical contact flow.

This document records the built static implementation in `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js` and the editorial pages generated by `scripts/build.mjs`. The approved visual reference is `.impeccable/mocks/decision/pick.png`, extended by the authorized connected-workspace hero. The supplied logo is authoritative. The workspace photograph is illustrative, not evidence of a customer project or company office.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Locally hosted Manrope with bold, tightly tracked headings.
- Bright surfaces, forest-green anchors and warm gold actions.
- Broad audience routes and thin-rule service lists.
- Flat page surfaces, restrained corners and consistent line icons.
- One connected-workspace scene with user-controlled motion.
- Explicit email draft preparation with no delivery claim.

## Colors

Forest green and gold carry the supplied identity; cool pale surfaces keep the service information calm and readable. Frontmatter records the reusable values; `style.css` remains the complete implementation source, including isolated divider and muted-text values.

### Primary
- **Forest green (`green`)** anchors the approach band, selected filters and icon/link treatments.
- **Deep forest (`green-deep`)** grounds the footer and gold-button text.
- **Warm gold (`gold`)** identifies primary actions, selected text and approach icons; `gold-hover` brightens buttons on hover.

### Neutral
- `white` is the main page and form background; `paper` is the contact backdrop and photo caption.
- `sage` distinguishes business routes, coverage, circular service links and draft results.
- `ink` is default text; `muted` is supporting prose; `line` draws quiet structural boundaries.
- `home-surface` distinguishes the residential route, with `home-hover` and `business-hover` for route feedback.

### Feedback
- `error` marks invalid controls and field messages.
- `focus` outlines links, buttons and disclosure summaries; inputs use a green focus outline.

## Typography

All roles use Manrope, Arial, sans-serif. One locally hosted variable WOFF2 (`assets/fonts/manrope-latin.woff2`) provides weights 400–800 with `font-display: swap`. There is no separate serif or monospace voice and no rigid modular scale.

- Hero display: frontmatter display style, maximum text width 680px. Desktop introduction is 18px/1.65 with a 500px maximum width.
- Section headings: headline style; approach and coverage use 43px, FAQ uses 37px. Route headings are 23px with -0.025em tracking.
- Service titles: 18px/1.15, weight 700; supporting descriptions are 13px with a 390px maximum width.
- Body: 15px/1.7. Section introductions are 16px, with maximum width 550px. Labels are 12px bold, with quieter optional labels.
- Controls: primary action 14px/1.5 weight 800; small header action 12px; navigation 13px weight 600.

Responsive type changes are explicit in CSS: hero 44px at 1200px, 39px at 980px, `clamp(34px, 7.7vw, 53px)` at 720px and 32px at 380px. At 1600px and wider it is 64px. Mobile form controls are 16px to remain readable and avoid small-field zoom behavior.

At 720px and below, service, audience-route, principle and FAQ descriptions use 14px; route topics, hero details, form instructions and contact hours use 12px; form labels use 13px. Footer text and links use 13px, with 11px legal text. The narrowest hero details use 11px. Editorial pages keep article prose and lists at 16px/1.85, with 19px summaries reducing to 17px on mobile.

## Layout

The centered container is at most 1440px wide, including 52px side padding. Padding becomes 32px at 1200px, 22px at 720px and 17px at 380px. Normal sections use 90px vertical padding, reducing to 58px on mobile. The hero uses 42px top and 45px bottom; the dark approach band uses 62px.

Desktop hero columns are 1.05fr/1fr with a 40px gap. The connected scene is 450px tall, including an 80px deep-green caption band; its photograph crops with `object-fit: cover` and `object-position: 50% 54%`. Audience routes use two equal columns with a 24px gap. Service rows form two columns with a 58px gutter. Contact and FAQ use .9fr/1.1fr with a 110px gap. The approach has four principles; coverage has two columns; the footer has three.

- At 1200px gaps and type tighten; the connected scene remains 450px tall.
- At 980px the scene becomes 410px tall, the header becomes 82px tall, the phone link hides, principles become two columns, coverage city links become one column and form rows become one column.
- At 720px the scene becomes 390px tall with a 76px caption band. The header becomes 76px tall with a menu control; major content grids stack. Coverage cities and form fields return to two columns within the now full-width sections. Footer brand spans two columns above navigation and contact.
- At 380px city and form grids become one column; the scene is 350px tall.
- At 1600px and wider the connected scene remains 450px tall.

Editorial service pages use a sage introduction with breadcrumb, headline, summary and quote action. Below it, a flexible article column (maximum prose width 780px) sits beside a 300px contact aside, with a 90px gap. At 980px the aside becomes 250px and the gap 40px; at 720px they stack and the aside stops sticking. Article sections use plain headings, paragraphs, lists, related-service links and a quiet sage quote panel.

The header is sticky at the top with z-index 20. Anchor scroll padding is 105px, changing to 90px on mobile. The skip link appears on focus above the header.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from alternating white, paper, sage and forest surfaces, thin rules and a modest button lift. The scene's two service labels alone use a subtle `0 8px 22px #042d2626` shadow to separate them from the photograph. There are no gradients or backdrop blurs. Header navigation overlays the page on mobile using an opaque white surface and bottom border.

State transitions use `220ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Buttons rise 2px on hover; text-link arrows move 4px; scene service labels rise 3px. The hero photograph drifts from scale 1 to 1.055 and translates up 3px over 16s, alternating with ease-in-out timing. SVG dashed connection paths flow over 5s with linear timing; connection-point rings pulse over 3.5s with ease-out timing, with the second delayed 1.1s.

**The One Focal Motion Rule.** Keep motion inside the connected scene; its caption and service-link text stay still. Manual pause, offscreen observation and document visibility pause the animations. Reduced motion disables animations and transitions, removes scene transforms and hides the motion control. No JavaScript also leaves the scene static and hides its motion control. Preserve these alternatives whenever the scene changes.

## Shapes

Use 8px primary-control and scene-label corners, 12px audience/form/editorial panels, 5px inputs, 16px scene corners and 6px menu/motion-control corners. The scene caption is flush inside its clipped outer frame. Filter pills use 30px radius; service-arrow hit areas are at least 44px circles. The email result uses 8px corners. Line icons use rounded caps and joins, no fill, generally 1.7px stroke; route/service icons use 1.5px strokes. Avoid turning the rule-separated service directory into a repeated card grid.

## Components

- **Primary action:** gold fill, deep-green text, 17px 25px padding, minimum 54px height and 24px icon gap. Header variant uses 13px 19px padding, minimum 48px height and 15px gap. Hover brightens and lifts. Disabled styling reduces opacity to .55 and uses a wait cursor; the current contact flow does not introduce a loading state.
- **Navigation:** desktop inline links; mobile button toggles `aria-expanded`, Menu/Close label and the visible link panel. Links, outside clicks, Escape and crossing to desktop close the panel. Escape returns focus to the toggle.
- **Audience routes and filters:** whole-panel home/business links filter service rows and navigate to the directory. Filters use `aria-pressed`; the selected filter becomes green. A polite status reports the visible count. Hidden rows use the `hidden` attribute.
- **Service and city links:** service titles open dedicated editorial pages; service arrows, scene labels and location links navigate to contact and prefill the corresponding select. Prefill clears that field's error and hides any stale draft result. Editorial quote links carry a known service selection back to the homepage form.
- **Connected scene:** two paper-colored service links sit over illustrative photography and cream signal paths. A deep-green caption band grounds the scene. The motion button reports its manual pause state with `aria-pressed` and a Pause/Play label. Forced colors hides decorative paths and points and adds control borders.
- **Editorial service template:** shared site navigation and footer frame a breadcrumb, service heading and summary. Readable sections explain coverage, preparation, expectations and common questions, followed by related services and a quote action. The green contact aside is sticky at 125px on desktop and becomes static on mobile. Privacy content uses the same editorial layout.
- **Contact form:** white bordered panel, 35px desktop padding, 12px radius. Name, phone, email, service and city are required; issue description is optional. Validation occurs on blur and on submit, with inline messages and `aria-invalid`. Invalid submission focuses the first invalid field. Phone accepts common separators with 7–15 digits; email uses the browser validity check.
- **Draft result:** successful validation constructs a quote request locally and reveals “Your email draft is ready.” No request is sent or stored by a backend. “Open email draft” is a `mailto:ahmed@thegeminfo.com` link with encoded subject and body; the visitor must review and send in their email app. “Copy request instead” copies the text; if clipboard access fails, a readonly textarea appears with selected text for manual copying. Editing the form hides the old result. Do not label this state as submission or delivery success.
- **FAQ:** native `details`/`summary` elements separated by rules. The plus rotates 45 degrees when open; answers stay compact and aligned to the text column.
- **Focus:** links, buttons and summary receive a 3px gold-brown outline at 5px offset. Inputs, selects and textarea receive a 2px green outline at 2px offset. Preserve native keyboard semantics and the skip link.
- **Brand and photograph:** header uses `assets/logo-header-light-bg.webp`, footer uses `assets/logo-header.webp`; both preserve the supplied mark. Use the generated `assets/workspace.webp` as illustrative imagery, never as customer proof.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Preserve the supplied Gem Infotech logo and its proportions.
- Use clear service language, visible contact details and the existing audience routes.
- Keep the service directory flat, separated by thin rules.
- Preserve keyboard focus, explicit validation and reduced-motion behavior.
- Describe the contact result as a draft requiring the visitor to send it.

### Don't
- Invent customer counts, rankings, testimonials or managed IT offerings.
- Replace the supplied logo with a generated approximation.
- Spread the scene-label shadow across page panels or add repeated entrance effects.
- Claim the contact request was sent or delivered.
